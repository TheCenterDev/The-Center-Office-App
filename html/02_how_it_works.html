How to Use This App

This app is your toolbox — it holds two kinds of things: Apps and Claude Skills. Here's what each one is and how to use everything else in the app around them.

Apps

Apps are the interactive tools you can open right from the sidebar — things like the credit card reconciliation tool. You'll see them marked with ↗ wherever they show up, and they're also all listed together on the Apps page (pinned near the top of the sidebar). Clicking one opens it in its own window, separate from the launcher, since it needs a real browser to run — the launcher stays open the whole time.

Claude Skills

Skills are the tasks Claude already knows how to do for this office — things like reconciling a bank deposit against QuickBooks or drafting the monthly budget-vs-actual financial summary email. There's nothing to open or click for these. Just tell Claude what you need in plain language — for example, "reconcile this deposit" or "draft the financials email" — and it takes care of it.

How to use this app

Signing in

The app opens to a sign-in screen. Enter your username and password (given to you by an admin) and click the blue arrow, or press Enter. There's an Admin account and a Staff account — what you see in the app is the same either way.

Home

Home is the first thing you see after signing in — a quick overview of what's in the app, plus a link out to the Employee Portal for anything that isn't a tool or Skill (onboarding info, policies, and the like).

The sidebar

Everything else lives in the sidebar on the left:

  • Home and Apps are pinned at the top and always visible.
  • Below them is the search box — type something and press Enter (or click 🔍) to find it anywhere in the app, not just titles in the sidebar. There's an × button to clear your search.
  • Below that is the list of guides, like this one — click any of them to open it in the main pane.
  • The arrow at the very top of the sidebar collapses it out of the way if you want more room; click it again to bring it back.

Settings

Settings is at the bottom of the sidebar. From there you can:

  • Switch between Light, Dark, Eye Comfort, or System (matches your computer) appearance.
  • Make the text Small, Normal, Large, or Extra Large.
  • Choose whether the app opens to Home or Apps by default.
  • Turn off remembering your username on the sign-in screen, if you'd rather it stay blank.
  • Reset everything back to defaults with one click.

Changes apply right away — no save button needed.

Refresh and Help

If a guide or app was just added or updated, click Refresh at the bottom of the sidebar to pick up the change without restarting the app. How to Use This Launcher, right above it, brings up this same kind of walkthrough any time you need a reminder.
