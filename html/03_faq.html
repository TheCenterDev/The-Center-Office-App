Frequently Asked Questions

The questions new office staff tend to ask first.

Who do I contact for IT issues?
Brad Boyles — Brad@thecentercc.com
Where do I find the employee handbook?
Check the Employee Portal, linked on the Home page.
How do I add or update a document in this launcher?
Drop an .html file into the html folder next to the app (or ask an admin to), then click Refresh at the bottom of the sidebar. Prefix the filename with a number, like 05_something.html, to control where it appears in the list.
An interactive tool didn't open — what do I do?
Restart the app. If that doesn't fix it, you may need the latest version — download it from GitHub.
