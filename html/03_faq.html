Frequently Asked Questions

The questions new office staff tend to ask first.

How do I get a password the first time?
You set it yourself. On the sign-in screen, type your @thecentercc.com email address and click Forgot password? — you'll get an email with a link to choose a password. Check your spam folder the first time; it comes from a firebaseapp.com address. Nobody hands out passwords, and there is no shared one.
Do I need a different password for the app, the website, and the tools?
No. One account works everywhere — the app on the office computer, the site on your phone, and every tool inside them. Sign into the app and the tools open without asking again.
How do I change my password?
Settings → Password, once you're signed in. The new one works everywhere immediately.
I signed in but it says I don't have access yet.
Your password is right, but nobody has added you to the team list yet. Ask a Director — it takes them about ten seconds on the Team page.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes. The address is thecenterdev.github.io/The-Center-Office-App — open it in your phone's browser and sign in with the same account. On an iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen, on Android use the menu → Install, and it behaves like an app. It's the same address for everyone, so it's safe to text to a coworker.
Who do I contact for IT issues?
Brad Boyles — Brad@thecentercc.com
Where do I find the employee handbook?
Check the Employee Portal, linked on the Home page.
How do I add or update a document in this launcher?
Drop an .html file into the html folder next to the app (or ask an admin to), then click Refresh at the bottom of the sidebar. Prefix the filename with a number, like 05_something.html, to control where it appears in the list.
An interactive tool didn't open — what do I do?
Restart the app first. If it still won't open, you're probably on an older version — ask Brad to run the updater, which pulls the newest build in one step.
