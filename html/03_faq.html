Frequently Asked Questions

The questions new office staff tend to ask first.

How do I get a password the first time?
You set it yourself, once your account exists. On the sign-in screen type your @thecentercc.com address and click Forgot password? — you'll get an email with a link to choose one. Check your spam folder the first time; it comes from a firebaseapp.com address. Nobody hands out passwords and there is no shared one. If it says the email and password don't match, or no email arrives at all, your account probably hasn't been created yet — ask Brad, it's a two-minute job.
Do I need a different password for the app, the website, and the tools?
No. One account works everywhere — the app on the office computer, the site on your phone, and every tool inside them. Sign into the app and the tools open without asking again.
How do I change my password?
Settings → Password, once you're signed in. The new one works everywhere immediately.
I signed in but it says I don't have access yet.
Your password is right, but nobody has added you to the team list yet. Ask a Director — it takes them about ten seconds on the Team page.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes. The address is thecenterdev.github.io/The-Center-Office-App — open it in your phone's browser and sign in with the same account. On an iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen, on Android use the menu → Install, and it behaves like an app. It's the same address for everyone, so it's safe to text to a coworker.

How do I add or update a guide in the app?
Ask Brad. Guides now live in the project itself, and publishing a change updates the app and the phone site together so everyone ends up on the same version. Editing a file by hand on one computer used to work, but only ever changed that one machine — which is exactly how the two versions drifted apart.
How do I get the newest version?
On the phone site, reload the page. On the office computer the app updates when the updater is run, so ask Brad if something you've been told about isn't there yet. The small number at the bottom of the sidebar on the phone site tells you which version you're on — handy when two people are looking at different things.
The phone site is missing something I know was added.
Reload the page — the site checks for a new version every time it's opened, so a reload is normally enough. If you've added it to your home screen, close it completely and reopen it. Compare the small version number at the bottom of the sidebar with someone else's if you want to be sure.
What's the bar at the top with a date in it?
A reminder from the Calendar: either something you saved, something on the whole team's calendar, or something someone put on yours. Dismiss it with the ✕ and it stays gone for the rest of the day, on that device. You choose how far ahead you get warned when you create the date.
Where are the Skills, and do I need them?
Skills in the sidebar. You don't need them to use anything here — they're for when you're talking to Claude somewhere else and want it to already know how this office does something. Download one, attach it to a Claude conversation, and then just ask in plain language.
An interactive tool didn't open — what do I do?
Restart the app first. If it still won't open, you're probably on an older version — ask Brad to run the updater, which pulls the newest build in one step.
